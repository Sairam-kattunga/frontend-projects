Project Zenith

A mindfulness app for the modern professional.

Client: Zenith Corp
Role: Lead Product Designer
Year: 2024
Tools: Figma, Sketch, Protopie

The Challenge

In a world of constant connectivity and overwhelming digital noise, professionals struggle to find moments of peace and focus. The challenge was to design a mobile application that provides accessible, effective mindfulness exercises without adding to the user's cognitive load.

My Process

My process began with extensive user research, including interviews and surveys with target users in high-stress jobs. This led to key insights about their needs for simplicity and brevity. I moved on to creating user flows and wireframes, focusing on an intuitive navigation system. Iterative prototyping and user testing were crucial in refining the UI and ensuring the experience was calming and not distracting.

The Solution

The final product is a clean, minimalist application that guides users through short, guided meditation sessions. It features a personalized dashboard, progress tracking, and curated content based on the user's mood and available time. The color palette and typography were carefully chosen to evoke a sense of calm and clarity.

The Results

Post-launch, Project Zenith achieved a 4.9-star rating on the App Store. User engagement metrics showed that average session times were 30% longer than initial projections, and user retention after the first month was 40% higher than the industry average for wellness apps.
